PRIVACY AND TRUST

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Patty ("we," "our," or "us") handles user information in connection with the Patty mobile application, the Patty web app, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

1. Data Collection and Use

Patty is a personal finance app, and your financial data belongs to you. We do not sell user data, and we do not use your financial information for advertising or profiling.

Patty works offline by default: transactions, balances, wallets, categories, savings goals, and budgets are stored on your device. The following information is collected or processed only in the specific circumstances described below:

  • Account information. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google in order to identify your account.
  • Synced financial data. If you enable cloud sync, your financial records and any profile image you upload are transmitted to and stored on our backend at pattyapi.landchit.dev so that they can be restored and kept consistent across your devices.
  • Purchase information. Subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing. We receive the purchase and entitlement information needed to determine whether your subscription is active. We never receive your payment card details.
  • Advertising data. The free version of the Android app shows ads. See section 4 for exactly what is collected and by whom.

2. On-Device Processing

Receipt scanning and the AI features that interpret your entries run entirely on your device. Receipt images are processed locally by on-device OCR and an on-device language model. Receipt photos are not uploaded to our servers, and they are not sent to any third-party AI provider.

3. Third-Party Services

Patty uses the following third-party services, each of which may process information under its own terms and privacy policy:

  • Google Sign-In — authentication.
  • Google Play Billing — subscription purchases and entitlement checks.
  • Google AdMob — banner advertising in the free version of the Android app, as described in section 4.

For details on how Google handles this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising

The free version of the Patty mobile app displays banner advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. To serve these ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it;
  • device information such as model, operating system version, and language;
  • interactions with the ads shown (impressions and clicks).

This information is processed by Google as an independent controller under its own terms, linked in section 3.

What we never send to advertisers

Patty does not share your financial information with Google or any advertising network. Your transactions, balances, wallets, categories, merchant names, savings goals, and budgets are never used as ad targeting signals, are never attached to an ad request, and never leave the app for advertising purposes.

Where ads appear

Ads are shown only in the Android app. The Patty web app displays no advertising.

Removing ads

Ads are not shown to users with an active Patty Cloud subscription.

5. Your Choices About Advertising

Consent. Where required by law, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, you are asked for your consent before any personalised advertising is requested. This request is presented by Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is loaded.

Advertising ID. You can review your ad settings, reset your advertising identifier, or delete it entirely from Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting it prevents apps from using it to build an advertising profile. These controls also let you opt out of ad personalisation on your device.

Subscription. Advertising is removed entirely for Patty Cloud subscribers.

6. Data Sharing Commitment

We do not sell user information, and we do not share it with data brokers or for unrelated commercial purposes. The only information shared with third parties is what is described in sections 1, 3, and 4: authentication data with Google Sign-In, purchase data with Google Play Billing, and the advertising data listed in section 4 with Google AdMob. Your financial records are never included in any of this.

7. Children

Patty is not directed at children. The Service is intended for users aged 13 and over, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Future Features

Patty may introduce future features requiring limited user identity information, such as a shared wallet capability. As of the effective date of this policy, shared wallets are not implemented. If and when they become available, Patty would only process the minimum information necessary to identify participating users and enable wallet sharing with the intended recipients.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect operational, legal, or product changes. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date, and material changes will be noted in the change log below.

10. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, including requests to access or delete your data, contact us at lance@landchit.dev.
